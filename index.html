Intelligent Attendance Analytics Platform

Fraud detection with hand-built algorithms: Rabin-Karp, Miller-Rabin, Aho-Corasick, edit distance, Dinic max-flow / min-cut, vertex-cover approximation

Record a check-in

Each check-in enters a queue and is analysed on arrival.

Attendance log with fraud scores

Alert threshold: a score above 70 is flagged for faculty.

Proxy group (min-cut)

Students linked by shared subnet and near-identical check-in time, separated from clean students by a min-cut.

Audit list (vertex cover)

A 2-approximation: the fewest students to audit so every suspicious pair is covered.

Reviewer assignment (max-flow)

Flagged records are matched to reviewers by specialty, two cases each.

How the engine works

Duplicate check: polynomial fingerprint of ID|session under two primes picked with Miller-Rabin. Pattern check: Aho-Corasick scans IP + device for blacklisted strings in one pass. Near-duplicate IDs: Levenshtein distance of 1 or less from the same IP. Proxy group: similarity graph, Dinic max-flow, residual reachable set is the min-cut side. Score: duplicate 60, blacklist 40, group 35, near-duplicate ID 25, capped at 100.
